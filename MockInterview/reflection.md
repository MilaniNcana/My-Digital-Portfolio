# Reflection: Mock Interview Video (STAR Technique)

## S – Situation
As part of the Digital Portfolio assignment for Project Presentation 3 (PRP370S), I was required to record a **mock interview video** and embed it in my GitHub portfolio. The purpose was to demonstrate my readiness for professional job interviews by simulating a real interview scenario. This exercise built on the Interview Skills training I had previously completed, where I scored 100/100 on the formative assessment covering interview techniques such as the CAR (Context, Action, Result) method.

## T – Task
My task was to plan, record, and present a mock interview video that showcased my ability to answer common interview questions confidently and professionally. The video needed to demonstrate proper interview conduct, structured responses, and clear communication. I also had to embed the video in my GitHub portfolio using Markdown or HTML so it could be viewed directly from the repository.

## A – Action
I prepared for the mock interview by reviewing common software developer interview questions, including behavioural questions like *"Tell me about yourself"*, *"What is your greatest strength?"*, and *"Describe a challenge you overcame."* I structured my answers using the CAR method to ensure each response had clear context, a defined action, and a measurable result. I also researched a target company to practice tailoring my responses to a specific role. I then recorded the mock interview, paying attention to my body language, eye contact, tone of voice, and professional appearance. After recording, I uploaded the video and embedded it into my portfolio using HTML within the Markdown file to ensure it rendered correctly on GitHub.

## R – Result
The mock interview video was successfully recorded and embedded in my GitHub portfolio. Through this process, I demonstrated and strengthened the following skills:

- Structuring professional interview responses using the CAR method
- Presenting myself confidently and clearly in a simulated interview setting
- Practising body language, eye contact, and professional tone
- Embedding multimedia content in a GitHub repository using HTML
- Preparing for real-world interview scenarios through structured rehearsal

This experience gave me practical confidence that goes beyond theoretical knowledge. By watching my own recording, I was able to identify areas for improvement in my delivery and refine my approach. I now feel significantly more prepared to perform well in real job interviews for software development roles.
